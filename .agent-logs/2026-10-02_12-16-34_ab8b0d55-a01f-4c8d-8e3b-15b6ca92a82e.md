Yes. **Every upload is capped at 50 MB**, whatever the format (MP3, WAV, MP4 and so on). The limit comes from Supabase's free plan, where recordings are stored. The upload dialog checks the size first and tells you to compress the file or paste the transcript instead.

How much meeting fits in 50 MB depends on the quality:

| Format | Size per hour | Fits in 50 MB |
|---|---|---|
| MP3, 32 kbps mono (speech quality; what I used for the 87-min AMI meeting) | ~14 MB | **3.5 hours** |
| MP3, 64 kbps | ~28 MB | about 1 h 45 min |
| MP3, 128 kbps (typical music export) | ~57 MB | about 50 minutes |
| WAV (uncompressed) | ~115 MB+ | under 30 minutes |
| Video, 720p | ~100 MB+ | often under 30 minutes, unless compressed |

So a normal one-hour meeting fits easily as an MP3. It's mainly WAV files and long videos that run into the limit.

**Ways to lift it, if you want:**
1. **Supabase Pro ($25/month):** you set your own per-file limit, up to many GB. This is the simplest real fix.
2. **Compress in the browser before uploading** (keep only the audio). This works on the free plan, but it adds a lot of code and slows uploads on phones.
3. **Leave it, and state it in the README** as a free-tier limit, with "Pro plan or audio-only upload" as the next step.

For the assignment I'd recommend **option 3**. It's a deliberate scope decision, and reviewers usually see that as good product judgement rather than a gap.
